About CardioSentinel | ECG Intelligence

Academic biomedical engineering platform for multi-lead telemetry and predictive arrhythmia alerting.

Project Overview

CardioSentinel | ECG Intelligence is an advanced central cardiac telemetry monitoring and arrhythmia research console. It simulates real-time 250 Hz dual-lead patient streams (Lead II and Lead V1) combined with the official PhysioNet MIT-BIH Arrhythmia Database (48 recordings, 360 Hz dual-channel benchmark).

Official Dataset Citation

Academic Citation: Moody GB, Mark RG. The impact of the MIT-BIH Arrhythmia Database. IEEE Engineering in Medicine and Biology Magazine, 20(3):45-50, May-June 2001. Online repository: https://physionet.org/content/mitdb/1.0.0/

Medical Standards & Standards Compliance

  • IEC 60601-1-8: Medical electrical equipment – Alarm systems in medical electrical equipment. Tiered crisis/warning alarm sounds and 120s snooze mechanics.
  • Pan-Tompkins Algorithm (1985): Real-time QRS detection with dual-threshold adaptive bandpass filtering.
  • Bazett's Formula: Rate-corrected QT interval calculation (QTc = QT / √RR).
Educational & Research Prototype Disclaimer: CardioSentinel is developed strictly for educational, scientific, and algorithm validation research. It is NOT an FDA/CE approved medical device and must NEVER be used as the sole basis for clinical diagnosis or life-critical medical interventions.
